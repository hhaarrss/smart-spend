2. What Data We Collect

SMS Content

If you grant SMS read permission, SmartSpend reads incoming SMS messages only from a whitelisted list of known bank sender IDs, such as HDFCBK, ICICIB, SBIPSG, and AXISBK.

All SMS parsing happens locally on your device. The original SMS text is not sent to our servers. The app extracts structured transaction details such as amount, debit or credit type, merchant or payee, masked account suffix, date, sender ID, and UPI reference when available.

We do not read, store, or transmit SMS messages from senders that are not on the bank whitelist.

Account, Transaction, And Usage Data

SmartSpend collects account information needed for sign-in, transaction and budget data you create or sync, and basic usage or crash diagnostics if enabled. [Confirm and specify analytics or crash SDKs before publishing.]

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To automatically detect and categorize transactions
  • To generate spending analytics, budgets, and insights

We do not use financial or SMS data for advertising, and we do not sell your data.

4. Data Storage And Security

Data is stored in PostgreSQL behind the SmartSpend backend API. Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Authentication uses JWT bearer tokens, and Google Sign-In is handled through Firebase Authentication.
